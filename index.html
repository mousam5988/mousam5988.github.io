From evidence to national policy

I lead vaccines marketing for Pfizer Saudi Arabia across Prevenar 20, Comirnaty and Nimenrix, including the national programme transition to Prevenar 20. Before that I spent four years leading Medical Affairs for the primary care portfolio, so my brand strategy starts from the evidence and from what decision-makers need to see.

My work sits where three disciplines meet. Pharmacy gives me the science of the product. Public health, from my MPH at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine, gives me population thinking and behaviour change. AI lets a small team produce evidence, insight and plans at a scale that used to need a much larger one.
